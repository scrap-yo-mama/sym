# Privacy

> Draft, to be reviewed by a lawyer before this site goes live. This text is not legal advice.

If the two versions differ, the French version prevails. The French text is the [privacy page in French](/fr/legal/confidentialite){.vp-raw}.

## Who is responsible

The publisher of this site is named in the [legal notice](/legal/legal-notice){.vp-raw}.

## What this page collects

Nothing. This page sets no cookie, runs no analytics, has no form and asks for no account or e-mail address. It loads nothing from a third party: fonts, scripts and images come from the same address as the page.

## What your browser keeps

If you choose a light or a dark theme, your browser keeps that choice in its local storage. This happens only after you have made the choice, uses no identifier and is sent nowhere. Your language is not stored: you choose it with the language link. The documentation pages of the same site have their own theme switcher: it keeps your preference in this same local storage as soon as they open, with the value “auto” (your system theme) until you choose, with no identifier and without sending anything. Whether this storage falls under article 5(3) of the ePrivacy directive is to be confirmed by a lawyer.

## What the host logs

This site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data when your browser asks for a page, such as your IP address, the time of the request and your browser's user agent. We do not receive these logs. GitHub's [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) describes what it does with them.

## Links to other sites

Links to GitHub, Render or Railway send a request to those services only when you click them. Their own privacy policies apply from then on.

## The software

SYM, the software, sends nothing to us: no telemetry and no update check. See the [Telemetry page](/explications/telemetrie){.vp-raw} (in French).

## Your rights

The GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification and erasure over the personal data that a controller holds about you. This site holds none. For any question, write to the address in the [legal notice](/legal/legal-notice){.vp-raw}.
